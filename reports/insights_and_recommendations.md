# Insights & Recommendations

## 1️⃣ Churn Behavior Insights

### Early Tenure Customers Are High Risk

* Customers with tenure below 6 months exhibit churn rates exceeding 45%
* Indicates gaps in onboarding, expectation setting, or early product engagement

### Contract Duration Drives Retention

* Month-to-month customers churn at nearly 4x the rate of two-year contract customers
* Longer contracts act as natural churn dampeners

### Revenue Risk Is Not Uniform

* High-value customers represent a smaller volume but a disproportionately high revenue risk
* Enables precision retention instead of mass discounting

---

## 2️⃣ Revenue & LTV Insights

### LTV Increases Non-Linearly With Tenure

* Customers surviving beyond the first year show sharply higher lifetime value
* Retention investments in early months have compounding ROI

### Pricing Alone Is Not the Root Cause

* Churn correlates more strongly with tenure and contract than absolute monthly charges

---

## 3️⃣ Cohort Analysis Findings

### First 3 Months Are Critical

* Steep drop-off observed in cohorts within first 90 days
* Retention stabilizes significantly after month 6

### Long-Term Cohorts Are Highly Stable

* Customers beyond 24 months show single-digit churn

---

## 4️⃣ Actionable Recommendations

### Short-Term (0–3 months)

* Improve onboarding flows
* Trigger early engagement nudges for new customers
* Monitor churn signals for first-time subscribers

### Medium-Term (3–12 months)

* Promote annual contracts for medium-LTV customers
* Introduce loyalty benefits post 6 months

### Long-Term (12+ months)

* Focus retention spend on high-LTV segments
* Build predictive churn models for proactive outreach
